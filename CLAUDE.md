## This project
- **What:** unofficial redesign concept of the Steam store, a portfolio case study. Not affiliated with Valve.
- **Newest home sections:** "Discounts and events" under the player panels (one big publisher sale, four smaller deals; choice and "Today"/"Midweek" labels in `src/data/events.ts`, no countdowns), then the discovery queue. Browse games has an Upcoming tab (`src/data/upcoming.json`, from `node scripts/fetch-upcoming.mjs`; not in search or `/browse`; shared fetch code in `scripts/steam.mjs`).
- **Game page** (`/game/[id]`, `src/sections/game/`, layout "media and buy box", chosen by the user; the right column is one short card, no cover art: price, "Add to cart" with a round Wishlist heart, "Send as gift" (paid, released games only; like the cart it only says it is a concept, no gift form), then the facts. From lg the card and Friends who play stick while the page scrolls): title and score; trailer (short loop, native controls, never autoplays) and screenshots beside a buy column (price, "Add to cart" that only says there is no checkout, local Wishlist, facts); About with tags; What players say; Friends who play; More from the developer; Games like this (shared tags, `src/lib/similar-games.ts`). Upcoming games have no buy button or reviews. Under About: the long description (first part, then "Read more") and, after the reviews, system requirements in a tab per system the game runs on (Windows, macOS, SteamOS and Linux; shared `src/components/tabs.tsx`), both plain text saved by `node scripts/fetch-details.mjs` in `src/data/details.json` (server only, `src/lib/game-details.ts`). Reviews rotate every 10s with previous, next and pause; they are made up, generic, and must stay labelled as made up (`src/data/sample-reviews.ts`). Never copy real players' reviews.
- **Stage:** home is built as layout A (sidebar, one lead game at a time (image left; badge on the image's top left; right: name, reviews, four landscape photos in two columns, then "Next game" with image, title and a filling line, 10s, pausable; dots below the card jump to any game; two columns in a padded card; the trailer loop plays over the main image on hover or keyboard focus (muted, no controls, no zoom; rotation waits; not on touch or with reduced motion), the right side has four screenshots and no video; featured games are chosen for their art and must have a trailer; the More details button slides a drawer up from the bottom to 68% over image and text, with the Wishlist and price bar staying below it (platforms as round icons; Escape closes, rotation waits); the name and pictures on the right take exactly the height of the main image; left column: rounded image, then description, genres and release date always visible, then Wishlist button (local only, resets on reload) and price on the right; no View game button, image and name are the links), player panels, tabs) with one pinned announcement as a slim row (small crop of the event art, title and date as text; closing it leaves a megaphone in the top bar) on top, a small "Send a gift card" link on the Featured title row (three close, slightly tilted blue cards, in front of a bar that starts under their middle, opening once on load and more on hover; links to a stub, no Valve logo on the cards), a blue discovery queue strip under the player panels, above Browse by category (text and a white "Open your queue" pill left, the whole strip is one link; behind it on the right two lines of large faded game images moving very slowly at 45 degrees in opposite directions, never the focus, stopped only by a quiet pause button; links to the `/queue` stub), then Browse by category (scrolling posters), The community recommends (one made-up review at a time: picture plus three small ones on the left, four in two rows between 1024 and 1279px; one column below 1024px; on the right name, Steam review score, studio and date, the quote in its own box, description, four tags, platforms, price and a local-only Wishlist), Browse games (tabs plus quick filters), From developers and publishers you know (three panels). `/browse` is the one filter page: filters live in the link (`src/lib/browse.ts`), each choice shows its count, phones get a bottom drawer. Section titles "Featured and recommended" and "Browse games", search at the top left and Steam's other sections as names in the top bar, sidebar and footer. Other screens, apart from the game page, are stubs (`[...slug]`). Swatches live at `/base`. Agree idea, content and layout as rough sketches the user can see before building any screen.
- **Dev server:** port **3300** (`pnpm dev`). 3000, 3001, 3100 and 3200 belong to other projects. Only one server for this folder.
- **Package manager:** pnpm. Checks: `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`.

## Must keep
- Visible "unofficial concept, not affiliated with Valve" notice on every page (`src/components/concept-notice.tsx`, text in `src/lib/legal.ts`) plus Valve's trademark line in the footer.
- `noindex` (layout metadata and `X-Robots-Tag` header). No lookalike domain.
- No working login or checkout, no forms that collect data.
- Neutral tone about the original store.
- Logo: official Valve artwork only, unmodified, with the ®, standing alone and small (Valve's rules forbid it as a primary or prominent feature). Rules: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/branding
- Motiva Sans (Steam's font) is not bundled. Typeface: Red Hat Text (confirmed). The floating Settings panel and `/settings` let a visitor switch to Onest or Sofia Sans (`src/lib/font-setting.ts`, saved in localStorage).
- No decoration: no stickers, tape, dividers, selective colour or tinting.

## Where code goes
- `src/app/globals.css`: tokens read from the live store on 2026-11-24, each with where it is used. No raw hex in components.
- `src/data/palette.ts`: swatch labels for the starter page; keep in step with `globals.css`. `src/sections/base/`: the swatch page.
- `src/sections/home/`: home. `src/lib/catalog.ts`: the only game data source (Zod-checked `src/data/games.json`, a saved snapshot of 150 games in euro from `node scripts/fetch-games.mjs`, run by hand, never at runtime; images load from Steam's CDN). `src/lib/announcement.ts`: the pinned strip.
- Logo: `public/brand/`, vector paths copied 1:1 from Valve's `steam_brandAssets.eps` (white version). Symbol in the closed sidebar and on phone, full logo with ® when open, both 44px high so the circle never moves or grows (the two files share height and origin). Opening: width and fades take 250ms; labels are always in the DOM on one line, cut off by the row.
- Search: `src/components/search/`, a centred modal (native `<dialog>`, blurred glass backdrop) opened by the top bar search, its icon on phone, or Ctrl/Cmd + K. Empty: popular searches as small cards and favourite categories as pills (`src/data/categories.ts`). Typing searches the local catalog only (`src/lib/search.ts`), nothing is sent or stored.
- Nav: `src/components/site-nav/`, items in `src/lib/navigation.ts`. Sidebar opens and closes on desktop (names as tooltips when closed); phones get the bottom bar. Three looks the visitor picks (`src/lib/sidebar-setting.ts`): floating dock (default), quiet (no lines, blue mark on the current page) and "Original" (key `classic`: the first sidebar as it was, round open/close button on its edge, group lines, round highlight, white tooltips, Settings at the bottom; keep it that way, only its icons are new); written as the dock plus `quiet:` and `classic:` variants. In the dock and quiet, open/close sits at the bottom and Settings is not a sidebar item: a floating button bottom right (`src/components/settings/`) opens a panel that changes the site live.
- Spacing on home: between sections 40px phone, 56px from md, 64px from xl (set once in `src/app/page.tsx`); title to content 12px phone, 16px from md; the announcement sits 24/32px above Featured. Controls beside a title must not make its row taller (`SectionHeader` pulls them in).
- Icons: @tabler/icons-react, filled.
- Shortcuts: Ctrl/Cmd + K search, Ctrl/Cmd + B open or close the sidebar (`useShortcut`). Print them only with `<ShortcutKeys>`: one plain quiet text such as ⌘K, like shadcn's menu shortcuts, no boxes and no separate keys. The user wants the ⌘ symbol on every system (Ctrl works too): in the search bar, the Settings panel and the sidebar toggle (always visible under it in dock and quiet; on hover in the original).
- Scrolling: sideways rows use Embla (`embla-carousel-react` with the wheel-gestures plugin). Up and down uses OverlayScrollbars, shown when the cursor moves: `<ScrollArea>` for panels, `<PageScrollbar />` for the page, one setting in `src/lib/scrollbar-options.ts`. No bare `overflow-y-auto`.
